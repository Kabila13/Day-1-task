About Our Company

WHERE IT STARTED

The early projects taught us how businesses work, how requirements change, and how much responsibility comes with building something people depend on.

GOING BEYOND WEBSITES

As the projects changed, so did our capabilities. We started going deeper into software development and broader technical solutions. Websites gradually became business applications, systems, integrations and more.

TIME FOR A NEW IDENTITY

By 2025, the work and the team had grown far beyond where we started. In December 2025, we decided it was time for the identity to grow too. That decision led to Creaviq.

CREAVIQ TAKES SHAPE

Planning for a physical office began in March 2026. The office opened in May. Creaviq Technologies Private Limited was incorporated in June 2026. A new chapter officially began.

STILL BUILDING

We've worked on everything from websites and business applications to AI-powered systems, cloud infrastructure, data solutions and blockchain projects.
